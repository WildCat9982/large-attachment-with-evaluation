Harbourline Freight Depot Operations Manual. This manual describes every depot in the Harbourline Freight network. Each depot has its own section, identified by a depot code, which names the depot manager and sets out the canteen hours, staff shuttle, parking, fire assembly point, dock doors, same-day dispatch cut-off, cold storage range and security desk extension for that depot. This edition covers 48 depots.


1. Silver Creek depot (HD-00001)

The Silver Creek depot (HD-00001) in Jakarta, Indonesia is managed by Lena Wong, who is the first point of contact for access passes, building issues and local safety arrangements. New joiners receive a site induction on their first morning, before they are allowed onto the warehouse floor.

The canteen at HD-00001 is open from 06:15 to 21:45 on working days and accepts the staff meal card. A free shuttle bus leaves the nearest station at 05:15 for the early shift and again eight hours later for the late shift.

There are 363 parking spaces at HD-00001, allocated on a first-come basis; those nearest the entrance are reserved for visitors and for colleagues with reduced mobility. If the fire alarm sounds, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point, which is the forecourt of loading bay 12.

The depot has 19 dock doors and 14 forklift trucks. The same-day dispatch cut-off at HD-00001 is 15:45; orders released to the floor after that time are dispatched on the next working day.

Cold storage is held at 0 to 4 degrees Celsius. The security desk at HD-00001 can be reached on extension 7578 at any hour, and safety shoes and a high-visibility vest must be worn at all times in the warehouse.
