POST-INCIDENT REVIEW
Warehouse management system outage, 14 July 2026
Prepared by the Site Reliability team. Status: final.

SUMMARY
On 14 July 2026 the warehouse management system (WMS) was unavailable for 1 hour 55 minutes in the early morning. During the outage three distribution centres (Tuas, Johor and Batam) could not process outbound shipments. In total 1,240 shipments were delayed. No data was lost.

TIMELINE (Singapore time)
02:10  Monitoring raised the first alerts for failed order messages between the WMS and the carrier gateway.
02:25  The on-call engineer, Priya Raman, was paged and began investigating.
02:50  The application servers were restarted. This did not help.
03:40  The root cause was identified.
04:05  A new certificate was installed and service was restored.
04:30  The backlog of queued orders had been processed and the incident was closed.

ROOT CAUSE
The outage was caused by an expired TLS certificate on the internal message broker. Once the certificate expired, the WMS and the carrier gateway refused to connect to the broker, so no order messages could be exchanged.

CONTRIBUTING FACTORS
The renewal reminder for the certificate had been sent to the mailbox of an employee who left the company in May, so nobody saw it. Our monitoring checked that the broker was running but did not check certificate expiry dates. The on-call runbook had no section on certificate problems, which slowed diagnosis by roughly forty minutes.

IMPACT
Outbound processing stopped at Tuas, Johor and Batam for the duration of the outage. 1,240 shipments missed their planned collection and were sent on the next available collection. Eleven customers with guaranteed delivery times were affected. Customer credits totalling SGD 18,500 have been issued.

ACTIONS
1. Automate certificate renewal for all internal services. Owner: Platform team. Due: 31 August 2026.
2. Add certificate expiry monitoring with a warning 30 days before expiry. Owner: Site Reliability team. Due: 31 July 2026.
3. Move all renewal reminders from personal mailboxes to the shared platform mailbox. Owner: Platform team. Due: 21 July 2026.
4. Add a certificate troubleshooting section to the on-call runbook. Owner: Site Reliability team. Due: 28 July 2026.

LESSONS
Checks that a service is running are not enough; we also need checks on the things a service depends on. Reminders that depend on one person's mailbox will eventually fail.
