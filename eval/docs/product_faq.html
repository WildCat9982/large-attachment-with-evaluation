Frequently asked questions

What is Harbourline Track?

Harbourline Track is a shipment tracking service for online retailers. It gathers tracking events from your carriers and shows every parcel in one dashboard, with branded tracking pages for your customers.

How often is tracking information updated?

Tracking events are refreshed every 15 minutes for all active shipments. Delivered shipments are no longer polled.

Which carriers are supported?

We support more than 40 carriers across Southeast Asia, including national postal services and the major express networks. New carriers are added on request for Enterprise customers.

What plans are available?

There are three plans. Starter costs $49 per month and covers up to 500 shipments a month. Growth costs $199 per month and covers up to 5,000 shipments a month. Enterprise pricing is agreed individually for higher volumes.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is required to start the trial.

Do you offer single sign-on?

Single sign-on with SAML is available on the Enterprise plan only. Starter and Growth accounts sign in with email and a password, with optional two-step verification.

Is there an API?

Yes. All plans include access to the REST API and to webhook notifications. The API is limited to 600 requests per minute for each account. Requests above the limit receive a 429 response.

How long do you keep my data?

Shipment and tracking data is kept for 24 months after delivery, then deleted. You can export your data at any time from the dashboard.

Are there mobile apps?

Yes, there are apps for iOS and Android. They show the same dashboard and can send push notifications when a shipment is delayed.

When is support available?

Our support team is available from 8am to 8pm Singapore time, Monday to Friday. Enterprise customers also have an emergency line outside those hours.
